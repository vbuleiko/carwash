Questions

Do I need WhatsApp Business?

No. It works with the normal WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business already on your phone. Messages go out from your number, so customers can reply to you directly.

My washers don't have smartphones.

They don't need one. One phone at the wash is enough — you pick who's washing from a list.

Is my customers' data safe?

Your data is only visible to you. We never sell it or message your customers ourselves. See our privacy policy.

Can the booking page be on my own website?

Yes. We can put your booking page and app on your own web address, in the look you choose — just ask us on WhatsApp.

How do I pay?

Monthly by EFT after your free trial. We'll remind you a few days before the trial ends.

See it with your own eyes

The demo is a pretend car wash with two weeks of history. Tap around — you can't break anything.
